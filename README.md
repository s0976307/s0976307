## Caquan Palmer

Computer and Information Sciences student at Niagara University, digital
forensics track.

I build AI systems that stay under human control: models propose,
policies decide what is permitted, and people keep authority over anything
irreversible.

### Projects

- **[mae](https://github.com/s0976307/mae)**: a personal AI copilot whose
  identity lives in written doctrine and external memory rather than in any
  one model. Includes an offline deployment (local Llama 3.1 8B with a
  deterministic safety guard), a policy engine that gates changes to the
  system itself, a privacy-first memory intake pipeline, and research papers
  on AI governance.
- **[eagles-classes](https://github.com/s0976307/eagles-classes)**:
  coursework in systems analysis and design, statistics, and history, plus
  research on AI in digital forensics and ALPR governance.
